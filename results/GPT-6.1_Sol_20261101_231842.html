An employee broke down crying in our one-on-one — he says he just can't cope anymore. I didn't see it coming. My next meeting is in 20 minutes, and I'm not a therapist. What do I do right now, this very minute? And what happens afterward?

Ответ модели

You don’t need to be his therapist. **Your job right now is to be calm, check his safety, and help him reach support.** You don’t have to solve everything in this meeting. ## Right now **1. Pause the work conversation.** Give him a moment, offer tissues or water, and say: > “Thank you for telling me. We can put work aside. You don’t have to explain everything right now. I want to understand what you need and make sure you’re safe.” Listen without trying to cheer him up, diagnose him, or immediately fix the problem. **2. Clarify what “can’t cope” means—including a direct safety question.** > “When you say you can’t cope anymore, what does that mean for you right now?” > “Are you thinking about suicide or hurting yourself?” Asking directly does **not** put the idea in someone’s head
