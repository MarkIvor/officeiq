Ответ модели

## Right now, in the meeting Your goal in this moment is narrow: **stay composed, concede what's true, and move the rest to private.** You do not need to win in this room — the room only needs to see that you can take a hit without crumbling or lashing out. The actual resolution happens later, one-on-one, where you have a real chance. Practically: - **Take a breath before speaking.** A few seconds of silence reads as composure, not weakness. - **Own the fair part specifically:** "You're right that the deadline slipped — that's on me, and here's how I'm fixing it." Conceding something true, precisely, signals security. - **Defer the unfair part without conceding it:** "On the other point, I see it differently and I'd rather walk through it with you directly than take up the team's time."
